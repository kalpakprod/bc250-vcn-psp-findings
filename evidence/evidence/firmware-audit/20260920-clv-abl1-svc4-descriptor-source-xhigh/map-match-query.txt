50:`0x1EF18` loops over row slots and enabled mask bits. It sends row+0x1C to register addresses `0x50278` and `0x52278`, row+0x20 to `0x5027C` and `0x5227C`, each plus `slot*0x100 + mask_bit*0x100000`. Helper `0x2A74(0,address,value,2)` implements the windowed 32-bit write. Values originate in the selected internal row, not the mailbox low byte.
