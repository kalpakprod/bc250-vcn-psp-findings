## Method

1. Enumerate current project state, focused corpus, tools, parsers, builds, and evidence.
2. Read the relevant source docs and historical claims to their ends; retain contradictions.
3. Hash exact inputs and parse outer SPI/PSP/$PS1/APCB/FV/FFS structures independently.
4. Extract selected executable firmware modules into a separate evidence area and load only those copies into CutterMCP.
5. Map VCN discovery, power/clock, memory/DFG, SMU/TOS, PSP/mailbox, and Linux-driver paths.
6. Form falsifiable hypotheses and perform the smallest host-side/static tests.
7. Produce a candidate design only as a new, unapproved file if the evidence justifies it; verify changed ranges/checksums independently.
8. Record unknowns, blockers, and the next decisive hardware test.
