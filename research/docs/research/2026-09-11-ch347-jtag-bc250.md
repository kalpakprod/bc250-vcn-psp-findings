## Findings and limits

- WCH documents CH347 silicon as supporting JTAG master mode with TMS, TCK, TDI, TDO, and TRST, plus vendor OpenOCD support. This is primary vendor source evidence for the chip family.
- USB identity `1a86:55db` and successful CH347 SPI operation do not prove that the operator's specific programmer PCB routes or labels the JTAG pins, nor that it supports 1.8 V I/O.
- BC-250 J2 is documented as an AMD HDT+/JTAG footprint at 1.27 mm pitch. CAD-derived VDDIO is 1.8 V. Do not connect a fixed 3.3 V adapter directly.
- A CH347 transport alone does not prove compatible AMD HDT+/CPU debug support. Initial use, if physically safe, would be a slow read-only TAP/IDCODE discovery with reset and write operations disabled.
