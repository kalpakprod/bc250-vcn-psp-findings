# D-R1 SMU second-core IPC result

Date: 2026-09-15 (Asia/Almaty)

## Conclusion

D-R1 closes the ROM-source provenance of the type-0x12 body but does not falsify second-core route functionality. The PMFW master sender is decoded and can carry firmware-call arguments `(address,value)` through operation `0xFE`; the exact type-0x12 source is confirmed to be the near-zero body found in `dump/2.bin`, while a receiver could still reside in unavailable on-die ROM or another runtime component. The Q3 `0x5C` VCN-adjacent path reaches the common write primitive but its statically derived domain-14 address is `0x0005C0FC`, outside the effective second-core forwarding interval `0x0005B000..0x0005B7FF` (lower comparison is exclusive). The second coherent `0x1B514` callsite (`0x24732`) has d=19/20/21 records selecting local-store targets `0x0006C8F8`; it does not provide an in-range IPC payload. A direct Q3 chain exists from msg `0x1D` through `0x2E6C8`, `0x2E448`, `0x2E481`, `0x23730`, and `0x246F4`; local `a2=0x13ED4` is established before the first loop read when the pre-loop `a8!=a9` branch enters, so the Q3-updated float fields are directly read. Which float-gated d values execute remains unresolved. No receiver-proven, Q3-reachable VCN payload route is established, but route functionality remains untested rather than disproved.

## Question and scope

Context: the BC-250 PMFW type-8 body and its type-0x12 companion, with VCN hardware attach still blocked after the parked runtime experiments.

Change under test: whether the internal second-core IPC sender (`0x1B514` -> `0x10D0`, operation `0xFE`) provides an untested, caller-controlled SMN route useful for VCN enablement.

Baseline: exact PMFW type-8 body `SMU_OFFCHIP_FW~0x8_0.58.6.0.body`, 262144 bytes, SHA-256 `5c805026581ace101ba43ef4d0f6b34354f4a61461a1c78d2fa6a335a53d9936`; existing Q3 table and the prior parked-runtime report.

Outcome: resolve sender payload control, enumerate only decoded callsites in `[0,0x3A000)`, test Q3 provenance, and identify whether the receiver effect is actually decodable.

Explicit scanner scope: `[0x0,0x3A000)` of the `0x40000`-byte body. The every-byte scanner returns instruction candidates, not CFG coverage; the two `0x1B514` matches (`0x2341C`, `0x24732`) were context-checked as coherent callsites. The scan omits `[0x3A000,0x40000)` and does not model indirect edges. Unresolved indirect edges are not counted as decoded callsites.

## Hypotheses and falsifiers

1. H1: operation `0xFE` is a usable second-core `(address,value)` write route because `0x1B514` forwards two firmware-call arguments. Falsifier: the sender does not preserve two arguments, or no receiver/transport exists anywhere in the runtime architecture. Result: sender payload is confirmed; the ROM corpus lacks the receiver, so route functionality remains unknown rather than falsified.
2. H2: a Q3-reachable VCN-related caller supplies an address in the forwarding range. Falsifier: the Q3 handler chain resolves only to an address outside `0x5B000..0x5B7FF`, while the other in-range caller remains indirectly reached. Result: Q3 `0x5C` resolves to `0x5C0FC` outside the range; the direct msg `0x1D` chain reaches `0x246F4`, local `a2=0x13ED4` makes the float-field alias direct on loop entry, and d/value-to-domain action remains float-gated.
3. H3: the missing receiver is merely a decoder gap in the type-0x12 image. Falsifier: the exact companion body is populated with executable bytes or a second-core handler table. Result: the body has only eight non-zero bytes, so this artifact cannot provide a receiver implementation; this does not exclude on-die ROM or runtime-shared code.

## Sender decode

`0x1B514` is a three-instruction wrapper after its entry: it stores outgoing arguments `a2` and `a3` at stack offsets `+0` and `+4`, loads operation `0xFE`, passes the stack pointer, and calls `0x10D0` [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/disasm-1b514.txt`]. It has no local register-address validation and no local SMN store.

`0x10D0` resolves its base pointer from the PMFW literal `0x01210600` (body literal storage at `0x170D0`); it writes the two payload dwords to base `+0x100/+0x104` (`0x01210700/0x01210704`) and writes `(operation & 0xff)<<16` to base `+0x118` (`0x01210718`). It sets a pending flag at `0x7AD4`, polls status bit31 without a timeout, copies the two payload dwords back to the caller pair, and returns the low 16 status bits [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/ipc-10d0.txt`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/dispatcher-extended.txt`].

The sender-side mailbox protocol is proven; the peer's operation-`0xFE` interpretation is not.

## Q3 msg `0x1D` and `0x2E448` closure

The validated Q3 handler at `0x2E6C8` reads its queue argument through `0xFFC`. `0x2E6D4` extracts `arg[31:16]` as `index`; `0x2E6D7`/`0x2E6DA` accept exactly `1..20`; `0x2E6DF`/`0x2E6E2` read the map entry at `*(0x13ED4 + 4*index) + 4`; `0x2E6E6`/`0x2E6E9` reject map types `0x13` and `0x14`. `0x2E6EF` extracts `arg[15:0]` as `value`, converts it to unsigned float, and `0x2E6FB` stores it at `*(0x13ED4 + 12*index + 0x140)`.

The direct call to `0x2E448` occurs immediately after `0x2268` and has no packed-argument move at `0x2E701`. The caller loads `0x40000` from literal `0x17084` for `0x2268`; windowed CALL8 maps it into the callee's incoming window, and the subsequent `0x2E45B mov.n a2,a7` overwrites the value before any loop use. In the coherent `0x2E448` body, `a7=0x13ED4` and `a5=0x13FC4` are literal-derived bases. The mixed-width stream decodes `0x2E451` (`3010fa`) as a 3-byte non-branch FP constant under GNU floating-point Xtensa configurations; pypcode lacks that constructor and records an explicit gap. Sequential length-driven decoding then reaches `0x2E454`, `0x2E456`, `0x2E458`, and `0x2E45B mov.n a2,a7`. The `0x2E45D` `a8!=a9` branch therefore enters the loop with `a2=0x13ED4`, before its first read at `0x2E468`; the `0x2268` outgoing `a10` is overwritten and is not the loop base. The loop uses `a3_i=0x13ED4+4*i`, `a2_i=0x13ED4+12*i`, terminates when `a2_i==0x13FC4`, reads `f2_i=*(0x13ED4+12*i+0x14C)`, and loads `d_i=*(0x13ED4+4*i+8)` at `0x2E46E`. `0x2E47F` passes `d_i` to `0x23730`; `0x23730` passes it to `0x246F4`. The 20 candidate d values are `[0xD,0x0,0x1,0x2,0x3,0x4,0x5,0x6,0x7,0xA,0x18,0x13,0x14,0x15,0x16,0x17,0xB,0x8,0x9,0xC]`, and `f2_i` reads `0x14020+12*i`, exactly the Q3-written fields for indices `i+1=1..20`. Branch comparisons can reduce which `d_i` reaches `0x246F4` [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/q3-msg1d-single-decoder.txt`, `q3-msg1d-provenance.txt`, and independent raw `/home/OWNER/orca/projects/orchestra/worktrees/home-kukuruza-orca-projects-bc250/sol-reviewer/.orchestra/tasks/8/raw-dr1-f7b7c43-checks.txt`].

## Q3 input provenance

- The Q3 table is at type-8 body offset `0x746C` (absolute SPI `0x90656C`); the equivalent inner-dump coordinate is `dump[0x8FF200] + 0x736C`, also SPI `0x90656C` [observed-bytes/static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/q3-selected-map.txt`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/stride-correction.txt`]. Message `0x5C` maps to handler `0x1F038`, and `0x5D` maps to `0x1F110`.
- Q3 `0x5C` handler `0x1F038` calls `0x2362C` with domain index `0x0E` at `0x1F0E6` and `0x1F0FD`; the corrected common-primitive field is `0xF92C` and its address source is `0x0005C0FC`, not the effective second-core interval [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/q3handler-1f038.txt`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/stride-correction.txt`].
- Q3 `0x5D` handler `0x1F110` calls `0x2362C` for domain indices `0x0F`, `0x11`, `0x10`, and `0x12`; corrected stride values remain outside the effective forwarding interval [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/q3handler-1f110.txt`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/stride-correction.txt`].
- Q3 msg `0x1D` directly reaches `0x2E6C8`; `0x2E701` directly calls `0x2E448`, `0x2E481` directly calls `0x23730`, and `0x23735` directly calls `0x246F4`. Msg `0x1D` splits the firmware-call argument into high16 index and low16 value, accepts indices `1..20` except map types `0x13`/`0x14`, and writes `ufloat(arg[15:0])` to `*(0x13ED4 + 12*index + 0x140)`. The coherent `0x2E448` body defines `a7=0x13ED4` and `a5=0x13FC4`; the mixed-width stream includes `0x2E451` bytes `3010fa`, which pypcode cannot construct but GNU floating-point configurations decode as non-branch `const.s f1,0`; the next decoded instructions are `0x2E454/0x2E456/0x2E458/0x2E45B`, and `0x2E45B` defines local `a2=a7` before `0x2E468`'s first loop read when `0x2E45D` takes the `a8!=a9` branch. The loop sets `a3=a7`, advances `a3+=4` and `a2+=12` at the backedge, reads `*(0x13ED4 + 12*i + 0x14C)`, and passes `d=*(0x13ED4 + 4*i + 8)` through `0x23730` to `0x246F4`. The read addresses equal Q3-updated fields `*(0x13ED4 + 12*(i+1) + 0x140)` for indices `i+1=1..20`. The candidate d sequence is `[0xD,0,1,2,3,4,5,6,7,0xA,0x18,0x13,0x14,0x15,0x16,0x17,0xB,8,9,0xC]`, subject to float-gated branch comparisons; direct d/value-to-domain action remains INCONCLUSIVE [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/q3-msg1d-single-decoder.txt`, `q3-msg1d-provenance.txt`].

## Receiver closure

The exact type-0x12 source is not a decoder artifact or an extraction mismatch: PSP entry index 4 points to SPI `0x93F700`, its first `0x40100` bytes match the imported signed source, and its `+0x100` 0x40000-byte body matches the imported body byte-for-byte [observed-bytes; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/receiver-provenance.json`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/type12-raw-comparison.txt`]. The full stock entry is `0x40200`; the imported signed source is only the `0x40100` prefix and leaves a `0x100` entry tail. The body has only eight non-zero bytes and Xtensa entry/call scans return no decoded entries or calls [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/body2-entries.txt`, `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/body2-calls-targets.txt`]. The type-0x08 master body is populated and contains the sender, but no bounded source scan found a receiver or an embedded type-0x12 pointer [static-analysis; `evidence/smu-ipc-d-r1/pmfw-embedded-source-scan.txt`]. Therefore the actual receiver effect is UNKNOWN, not “writes an SMN register”; route functionality is not falsified by the absent ROM receiver.

## Findings and confidence

- **F1 — `0x1B514` forwards two dwords supplied by its firmware caller under operation `0xFE`.** CONFIRMED — primary evidence is direct Xtensa disassembly of the exact hash-pinned body; this is not operator-controlled input.
- **F2 — `0x10D0` uses payload locations `0x01210700/0x01210704` and status/command `0x01210718`, with a no-timeout bit31 poll.** CONFIRMED — primary disassembly plus direct little-endian literal reads.
- **F3 — the exact dump type-0x12 entry and imported type-0x12 body are byte-consistent and contain no receiver implementation.** CONFIRMED — raw PSP entry, exact dump slice, imported source, body hash, and independent decoder scan all agree; an on-die/runtime receiver remains possible.
- **F4 — Q3 `0x5C` reaches the common domain path but its corrected domain14 target `0x0005C0FC` is outside `0x5B000..0x5B7FF`.** CONFIRMED for static address provenance — decoded handler and corrected table arithmetic; no hardware effect inferred.
- **F5 — `0x24732`'s corrected d=19..24 records all take local-store branches with targets `0x0006C8F8` or `0x0006D0F8`.** CONFIRMED as static payload control — corrected stride and branch condition; no second-core payload is emitted by these paths.
- **F6 — Q3 msg `0x1D` has a direct transitive chain to `0x246F4`, and its updated float field is directly consumed on loop entry.** CONFIRMED as direct static call and address-provenance reachability — coherent handlers, recovered mixed-width boundaries, local `a2=a7=0x13ED4`, and direct calls establish the chain; float comparisons still leave the executed d/value action INCONCLUSIVE.
- **F7 — The Q3-updated float field is read directly by `0x2E448` after local `a2=a7=0x13ED4` on the `a8!=a9` loop-entry path.** CONFIRMED — mixed-width boundary recovery and pypcode at `0x2E45B` establish the local definition; GNU cross-decoder evidence preserves the pypcode gap at `0x2E451`.
- **F8 — an actionable receiver-proven VCN route is not established.** CONFIRMED as a bounded static coverage result — the ROM source does not contain the receiver, while float-gated d/value action and indirect coverage remain unresolved; route functionality is not falsified.

## Counter-evidence and limits

- The sender's no-timeout poll is a real denial-of-service hazard, but it is not evidence that the peer accepted or executed the request.
- The corrected `0x24732` d=19..24 paths prove only local state/table address selection, not receiver semantics or a VCN register effect.
- The empty type-0x12 body is a confirmed stock source slice, not proof that the second-core receiver does not exist in on-die ROM or another runtime component.
- Static Q3 table reachability cannot settle indirect registration/callback edges. Those are explicitly INCONCLUSIVE, not counted as decoded callsites.
- No hardware command, MMIO access, mailbox access, firmware write, dependency install, or image modification was performed.
