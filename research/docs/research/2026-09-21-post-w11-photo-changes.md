## Finding and limit

**Finding:** no retained post-W11 _user-board_ photo or marking reference in the reviewed records proves a later PCB revision, the populated `PUS1` or `PUA1` package text, or any change to either designator/component. The post-W11 references that name these parts are community annotations or static CAD/GenCAD labels; neither is revision-matched to the operator's physical board. No before/after pair tied to the same board exists in this evidence set.

This is a negative limited to the named, retained records. It does not establish that the board was not physically modified or that no additional photo exists among unreviewed media. Actual revision, current component population/top markings, and whether any physical change occurred remain **unknown**. Evidence class: `observed-bytes` for cited report/file identity and dates; `historical-report` / prior visual inspection for image content; no new `hardware-verified` result.
