## Conclusion

The exact CLV HII packages expose three distinct groups:

1. **CbsSetupDxe AMD CBS/SMU/UMC controls**: VDDCR offsets, VMEMP enum, BTC/AVFS/DLDO margin controls, and telemetry calibration fields.
2. **Setup voltage configuration**: VCORE/GFX/MEMIO fixed-voltage and loadline controls in a separate `Setup` VarStore; the voltage form is hidden/gray in the parsed package.
3. **Display-only voltage names**: Hardware Monitor text such as CPU Vcore, VDDC_GFX, VDDC_SOC, and VMEMIO; these are not controls and are excluded from the 39-row inventory.

No field can be translated into a safe SoC voltage from this evidence. User `1.15 V` remains a requested ceiling/constraint, not a board-validated rating. The Cbs `VDDCR_VDD` and `VDDCR_GFX` fields are offsets/values with unresolved rail, consumer, unit, and sign semantics. Their IFR help says signed `[mV]`, but the numeric IFR bounds are `0..0xFFFFFFFF` and no negative option/range is present. Telemetry fields use a separate sign enum plus nonnegative magnitude; this does not establish the voltage-offset encoding.

## Encoding and signedness

- **VDDCR_VDD Offset** (`QID 0x36C`, `+0x6E6`, `u32`, donor `00000000`) and **VDDCR_GFX Offset** (`QID 0x36E`, `+0x6EB`, `u32`, donor `00000000`) have IFR numeric bounds `0..0xFFFFFFFF`, step `0`, and default zero. The help text calls the VDD field a signed integer `[mV]`, but no negative range or sign bit is exposed by IFR. Do not assume two's-complement, mV, absolute voltage, or SoC rail.
- **Telemetry offsets** (`+0x773`, `+0x77C`, `+0x785`) are `u32` magnitude fields with separate `Positive=0` / `Negative=1` sign questions (`+0x772`, `+0x77B`, `+0x784`). This is explicit sign/magnitude for telemetry calibration, not proof of voltage-offset encoding.
- **Setup Voltage(mV)** (`QID 0x74` and `0x77`) are `u16`, bounds `700..1250`, step `1`, donor value `900`; the `MEMIO Voltage` enum (`QID 0x7A`) offers `1.20..1.45 V`, donor `1.35 V`. These are separate Setup fields, hidden/gray, and not mapped here to BC-250 SoC/GFX physical rails.
- **VMEMP Voltage Control** (`QID 0x271`, `+0x37C`) is an enum with labels `0.7..1.1V` and `Auto`; donor value is `0xFF Auto`. The labels do not prove rail identity, application, or a safe range.
- `CPU/GFX DC BTC Set voltage in mV` and error offsets are `u16`, nonnegative bounds `0..65535`; BTC mode controls are separate enum bytes. No consumer or applied voltage is proven.
- AVFS/DLDO/DFLL/telemetry controls are voltage-adjacent calibration or feature controls, not decoded absolute-voltage setpoints.
