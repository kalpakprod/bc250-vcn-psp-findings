## What the document establishes

1. Section 11.1, printed page 75: top marking **25Q128JVSQ** maps to **W25Q128JVSIQ**, 128Mbit, SOIC-8 208mil. Capacity is 16MiB.
2. Section 11, page 74: V = **2.7–3.6V**, S = SOIC-8, Q = **QE=1 (fixed)**. Footnote 5: `/HOLD function is disabled to support Standard, Dual and Quad I/O without user setting`.
3. Section 7.1.4, page 16: IQ/JQ have factory-fixed QE=1; IO2/IO3 enabled, `/HOLD` disabled. Generic read/write wording of the QE section is not permission to treat the fixed-Q ordering variant as programmable IM/JM.
4. Sections 8.2.21–22, pages 44–45: B9h enters power-down after CS rises; only ABh is recognized in power-down. ABh restores normal operation; power-up starts in normal standby. Power-down is not a persistent physical disconnect and can be defeated by an ordinary host ABh transaction.

## Consequences for the current implementation

- **The current HOLD/MISO rig is not applicable to this populated chip.** Driving its pin 7 low must not be represented as pausing the flash or releasing its output: on this variant that pin is IO3, not an enabled HOLD input.
- Hardware activation stays default OFF/fixed-false. Do not enable `rig_init`/`rig_arm`, connect GPIO20 as HOLD, or attempt to clear QE as a workaround.
- Existing host/C guard tests establish code behavior at a fake peripheral boundary, not flash isolation. They cannot make IO3 become HOLD.
- A clip/J4004 connection does not break the existing flash-output connection. Simultaneously driving the same MISO with RP2350 can cause contention.
- DPD remains a **candidate requiring a separate proven plan**, not an accepted no-solder solution: safe ownership of CLK/MOSI/CS during B9h, confirmed FCH reset/tri-state behavior, ABh handling, and a full compatible responder while the original is asleep are not established. The current 4KiB one-window backend is not such a full responder.
- Current responder first-bit timing remains an additional blocker (`rig_timing.py`: ten-cycle optimistic matched path); identifying the flash does not repair it.

## Remaining evidence and permissions

Exact-chip uncertainty is reduced by owner confirmation and the manufacturer's marking table. No live register, electrical level, flash-release or FCH-reset measurement has been made. `PROGRAM#`/J4004 is not yet a proved safe master-isolation control. No BC250 commands, QE writes, B9/AB, reset, pin lifting, wiring, firmware flash or purchase was performed or authorized by the identity confirmation.

This rules out the HOLD-based route for the identified Q variant. It does **not** prove all possible no-solder routes impossible; it also does not justify declaring mandatory PSP/TOCTOU complete.
