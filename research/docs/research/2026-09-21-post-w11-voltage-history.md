## Classification by requested change type

- **VRM feedback:** one direct operator-reported adjustment, dated 2026-09-05, **before W11**. No later operator statement found in the sanitized records reviewed.
- **Resistor / physical modification:** no post-W11 resistor value, reference designator, rework description, or physical-modification confirmation found. The older “feedback adjusted” wording is not enough to identify a particular resistor or prove a specific solder/rework operation.
- **SoC values:** `~1.2 V` is a self-report on 2026-09-05, not an instrument record; `1275/912` are 2026-09-13 firmware telemetry words with unresolved rail identity. Neither establishes post-W11 voltage. `1.15 V` is recorded in later documents as an operator constraint/target, not an observed value.
- **Voltage offsets / BIOS fields:** saved W11 bytes show zero VDDCR_VDD and GFX offsets. Later project records analyze the fields and consumers, but the reviewed post-W11 action logs document no BIOS/NVRAM change. The offset bytes do not identify SoC or prove an absolute voltage.
- **After-W11 status:** no post-W11 change is documented in the checked record set; actual post-W11 physical/NVRAM state remains **unknown**. Do not convert this bounded absence into “operator confirmed no change.”
