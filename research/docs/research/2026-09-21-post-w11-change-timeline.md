## Change categories: what is and is not evidenced

- **Firmware/SPI:** W11 is explicitly reported as a flash/readback at its own capture. In the selected later records, no subsequent flash/write command, post-W11 full-chip readback, or later installed image hash is documented. The 2026-09-20 “still holds W11” line is a project status report, not fresh byte readback. Therefore the current firmware cannot be stated as W11 or as any other image.
- **NVRAM/CMOS:** The NVRAM ledger decodes the W11 capture and older snapshots. It documents no post-W11 Setup/NVRAM readback or explicit CMOS clear. A boot may write variables, but the retained W12 dmesg does not establish a variable write and no subsequent byte capture measures one; whether a clear or NVRAM change occurred is unknown.
- **Physical board/VRM/feedback:** The saved voltage ledger's participant report of manual feedback adjustment and self-reported “1.2-something” SoC is dated 2026-09-05, before W11 and not tied to an identified board/firmware/instrument. The selected post-W11 records document no later physical VRM/feedback change. Current physical state is unknown.
- **Offline artifacts:** Candidate ROMs and research outputs created after the W11 file are not proof of a board flash. The 2026-09-20 FD-port action log explicitly identifies its candidate as offline-only/unflashed. The 2026-09-21 ledgers are read-only. Do not equate a candidate-file modification with a physical SPI/NVRAM change.
- **Operator statement:** “да менял” establishes that the operator reports some change since the queried W11 reference, but the conversation answer does not name the change or its date. This is `historical-report` / operator statement, not a byte-level readback.

## Reproduction and limits

This audit reused the paths and findings in the named W11, boot, ROM, NVRAM, voltage, and candidate-build ledgers. It recomputed hashes for the decisive saved artifacts and used a bounded date/keyword review of the indexed action-log/report references for W11 and change terms; it did not scan the imported corpus, other evidence trees, or raw chat. The observed W11 and W12 filesystem metadata were checked with `stat`; file mtimes are not treated as event times.

**What is supported by the saved records:** the saved W11 readback and its candidate identity hash; W11's stored NVRAM bytes; a partial W12-labelled Linux VCN-init failure log; a 2026-09-20 project status that reported W11 remained installed; and the earlier-than-W11 2026-09-05 participant feedback-adjustment report. Separately, the operator has currently stated that some change was made, without specifying what or when.

**What is not proven:** which post-W11 change occurred, its date, whether it altered firmware, NVRAM/CMOS, or physical hardware, any present chip contents, any current Setup values, or current SoC/VRM voltage. A current identity/NVRAM readback or a dated, specific operator change record would be needed to bind a change to an artifact; neither is present in this bounded source set.

No hardware operation was authorized or performed by this audit. No source image, primary capture, candidate, memory file, or board state was changed.
