## What this changes in our implementation

1. Controller functionality must remain an adaptation of DS5Dongle, not a new BT/HID/audio rewrite.
2. Host-side 40-CU/UMR/control-center projects are candidates for read-only metrics and UI only. Their register writers are outside the safe adapter scope.
3. Existing BIOS/J4004 documentation improves pinout provenance but does not prove bus isolation while FCH is running. The adapter's SPI transport remains disabled by default.
4. VCN research currently supports a split conclusion: direct driver-side firmware loading may be a separate avenue, but SMU prerequisite/fabric-gasket barriers remain unresolved. It does not authorize PSP signature replacement or a runtime TOCTOU bypass.
5. The `simpmix` project is a research lead for software fallback, not a reason to claim VCN hardware is enabled.
