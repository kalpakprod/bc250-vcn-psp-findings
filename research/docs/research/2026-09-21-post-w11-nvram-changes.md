## Method correction

One secondary display-only helper initially called `parse_records` across the full 16 MiB image instead of the bounded NVAR store and therefore reported a non-NVAR gap after the NVAR region. This was not the actual `nvar_audit.py` invocation (which bounded the region); rerunning the helper on `raw[:0x20000]` produced no parse errors and the field/body hashes above. The erroneous full-image helper result is not used as evidence.

The reopened W11 `AmdSetup` record is at `0x4BB`, and its body starts at `0x4CF`; therefore the literal `UclkDiv1M0` byte at body-relative `+0x330` is at absolute `0x7FF` (not `0x7FC`). The same result is reproduced by this report’s bounded byte reader and candidate build manifests. `0x7FC` is the absolute location of `BankGroupSwap` at `AmdSetup+0x32D`. The earlier report `docs/research/2026-09-21-baseline-nvram-ledger.md` currently labels `UclkDiv1M0` with absolute offset `0x7FC`; it should be corrected by its owner. This worker was instructed to own only this report and its action log, so the earlier report and shared memory were not edited.
